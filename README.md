# SKIN111 – Homepage Redesign

## 1. Website Overview

SKIN111 Medical Wellness is a multi-location medical aesthetics and wellness provider established in Dubai in 2007, serving customers across Dubai and Abu Dhabi. Its services include aesthetic treatments, advanced laser procedures, cosmetic surgery, skin rejuvenation, weight management, wellness and longevity programmes.

The redesigned homepage focuses on communicating SKIN111's broader medical wellness positioning, making its extensive treatment portfolio easier to explore and presenting professional credibility more cohesively. The design direction prioritises a premium healthcare identity, structured navigation and a clearer journey towards consultations and appointments.

## 2. Main Improvements

The redesign addresses three key issues identified on the original website:

### 1. Unclear Brand Positioning
- Refined the homepage messaging to communicate SKIN111's wider expertise across medical aesthetics, wellness, longevity and specialist care.
- Strengthened the introduction to reflect the company's broader healthcare offering rather than focusing primarily on aesthetics and anti-ageing.
- Created a clearer brand message that better represents the full scope of its services.

### 2. Overwhelming Treatment Navigation
- Reorganised the extensive treatment portfolio into clearer service categories.
- Improved the presentation of treatments to help visitors explore options according to their individual concerns and goals.
- Established a more structured content hierarchy to make treatment discovery easier.

### 3. Disjointed Trust and Conversion Journey
- Improved the organisation and presentation of doctor profiles, specialist credentials, clinic information and patient reviews.
- Brought credibility information into a more cohesive homepage structure.
- Strengthened the connection between trust-building content and consultation or appointment pathways.

## 3. Design Improvements

- **Visual Presentation:** Developed a more cohesive premium medical wellness aesthetic aligned with SKIN111's positioning.
- **Layout and Structure:** Improved section hierarchy and treatment organisation to accommodate the company's extensive service portfolio.
- **User Experience:** Created a clearer journey from brand introduction to treatment discovery, credibility information and appointment pathways.
- **Content Consistency:** Established a more unified presentation of medical services, wellness offerings and professional information.
- **Overall Appearance:** Improved the visual organisation and balance of the homepage to present SKIN111 as a comprehensive medical aesthetics and wellness provider.

## Project Summary

The redesigned homepage addresses three major challenges: unclear brand positioning, overwhelming treatment navigation and a fragmented trust and conversion journey. The result is a more structured and cohesive digital presentation that communicates SKIN111's wider medical wellness offering while making treatment discovery and appointment exploration more straightforward.
